# Exploratory analysis: exp-declined-feature-requests

**Not pre-registered.** Notes made after the review.

- **Highly upvoted declined feature requests are rarer than expected.** Across 82 popular developer-tool repositories, the top 10 "not planned" issues each (668 in total) held only 31 feature-like requests with at least 20 upvotes, in just 9 repositories. Most "not planned" closures are bugs, questions, or requests with few votes.
- **"Not planned" often doesn't mean declined.** 3 of 9 were closed by bots or in bulk clean-ups, not by a decision against the feature.
- **Where a declined feature can be built as an add-on, free add-ons appear quickly.** Gemini CLI's Neovim integration has at least five free community plugins, and herdr's worktree layout has two. This matches the free, sponsor-funded model of Refined GitHub.
- **Nobody offered to pay.** Not one of the 9 threads had a comment offering money, even where users were clearly frustrated (Bruno, Dokploy).

Interpretation: developers fill each other's gaps for free and fast. A developer audience combined with small, add-on-sized gaps is a poor source of paid products. Ideas with a small money story probably need an audience that already pays for tools to save time (small businesses, freelancers, teams), rather than individual developers.
